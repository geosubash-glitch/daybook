Delete your Daybook account

For Daybook, by Geo Subash

You can erase your account and everything in it yourself, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Daybook and sign in.
  2. Tap the settings icon at the top right.
  3. Scroll to Delete account and tap Delete my account and journal.
  4. Confirm with your password or Google, then tap Erase everything.

This permanently deletes every entry, photo and setting, and your sign-in, straight away. It cannot be undone, so make a backup first if you want a copy (Settings, then Back up everything).

Cannot open the app

Email geosubash@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Daybook account". Your account and all its data will be erased within 30 days, and I will reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your journal entries, photographs, preferences, passcode check, any Gemini key you saved, and your sign-in record. Nothing is kept afterwards. Google's routine backups may hold deleted data for a short period before they expire. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
