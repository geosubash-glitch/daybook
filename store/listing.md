**Full description**

Daybook is a quiet, private journal. Open it and you are on today's page. Write as much or as little as you like, any time of day, and every day gets its own dated page that stays with you for life.

A page for every day
Opens on today. Move back a day, or jump to any date in any year with the calendar. See what you wrote on this day in earlier years.

Write anywhere, even offline
Keep writing with no signal. Your words are kept on the device and upload on their own when you are back online.

Tidy up as you write
Fix spelling and grammar on demand, or let Daybook do it as you go.

Print or save any stretch of your life
Choose any days, then print two days to an A4 sheet with every day dated, or save plain text with only your words and their dates.

Private by design
Sign in with Google. Your journal sits in a private area only your account can open. Add a passcode lock. No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.
